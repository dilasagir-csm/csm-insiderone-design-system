MetricStat is a big-number stat block for proof-point moments ("1500+ Top Global Brands", team/office/customer counts).

```jsx
<MetricStat value="1500+" label="Top Global Brands" />
<MetricStat value="27" label="Global Offices" tone="dark" />
```

Variants: `tone="light"` (Solar-colored number, for Nova/white backgrounds) and `tone="dark"` (cream number, for Eclipse/Dark Matter backgrounds). Typically laid out in a row of 3–4 across a metrics slide.
