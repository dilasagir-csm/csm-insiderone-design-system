JourneyNode is one pill-shaped step in an Architect journey-builder flow diagram, colored by element family.

```jsx
<JourneyNode type="starter" label="Sign-up not completed?" />
<JourneyNode type="wait" label="2 minutes" />
<JourneyNode type="channel" label="WhatsApp" />
```

Variants (`type`): `starter` (Solar), `wait` (Lunar), `channel` (Orbit), `action` (Celestial), `check` (Eclipse). Chain several left-to-right with a small gap to recreate a flow diagram.
