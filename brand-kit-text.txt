The Insider logo is a critical element of our brand identity. To maintain consistency and professionalism across your materials, please follow these
guidelines when incorporating the Insider logo into your content.
1. Use the Approved Logo in the Footage Below
○ The Insider logo shown below represents the correct version for use in your presentations.
○ Do not modify the logo in any way—avoid altering colors, proportions, or adding any effects (e.g., shadows, outlines).
2. Copy and Paste for Consistency
○ To ensure accuracy, copy and paste the logo directly from the footage below to your presentation slides.
○ Refrain from re-creating, resizing disproportionately, or distorting the logo in any way. Use only the official version at its designated
size and resolution.
3. Logo Placement
○ If a partner’s logo is included on the page, the Insider One logo should be placed on the left, and the partner’s logo on the right.
○ If no partner logo is included, the Insider One logo should be placed on the right side of the page.
4. Appropriate Backgrounds
○ Ensure the logo remains clearly visible by placing it only on simple, clean backgrounds. Avoid using the logo on busy, textured, or
complex backgrounds that may obscure or detract from it.
By following these guidelines, you’ll help maintain a cohesive, professional look that strengthens our Insider's recognition and impact. Thank you for
your attention to these details!
